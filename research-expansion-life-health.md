[1]

General illness waiting30days; specified conditions120days, measured from coverage/increased-benefit approval as applicable. Copayment is not permanently zero: renewal triggers include ≥3 minor-illness admissions and ≥200% claim ratio, or ≥3 qualifying general-illness admissions and ≥400%; next-year30%, both triggers50%, reviewed annually. Exclusions include congenital conditions, cosmetic treatment and pregnancy/fertility treatment. Locator: brochure PDF pages3,9–10 (1-based), waiting/exclusions and renewal sections. [2]

**Unknowns:** actual premium, main policy identity/cost, applicant underwriting, full geographic exceptions and selected endorsements. ## Data integration rules from these findings

1. Preserve actual identity, insurer legal entity, tier and channel; life insurer and similarly named general insurer are distinct.
2. Known cells need the exact source/locator above. Monetary cells retain basis: annual, per-confinement, per-illness, per-accident or benefit formula.
3. Quotes and rider-only premiums cannot be declared within a total budget. Do not fill unselected deductibles/OPD with maximums. AIA and BLA AccidentCare require main policy cost disclosure.
4. Life sum-assured percentages are formulas, not investment yields. Death/terminal triggers are not automatically additive. PA hospital lump sum is not daily income.
5. Available online/contact buttons establish a currently published sales channel only; contract/version/underwriting remain material. Store `checkedAt=2026-09-12`; unknown source publication/effective dates stay null.
6. Research readiness differs from completeness: six candidates have substantive core tables/conditions; FWD remains partial. All seven may be represented honestly only if unverified fields stay unknown and the UI exposes dependencies. No app/catalog changed by this report.
