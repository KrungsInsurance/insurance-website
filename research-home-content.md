# Home content: insurance basics and official campaigns

Research reference date:12September2026 (2569), Thailand. Primary-source discovery across14insurer brands, followed by detailed review of4date-valid campaigns. Three campaigns have original official image URLs ready to use; the fourth has verified terms but image retrieval blocked. This is a bounded company/campaign scan, **not every Thai insurer or every product**. Campaign validity means the published interval includes the reference date, not guaranteed eligibility or remaining stock.

## Integration constraints

Campaign metadata belongs in a Home content record with insurer, sourceURL, officialImageURL, startsAt, endsAt, checkedAt and eligibility; do not alter canonical product premiums/benefits using campaign maxima. Use expiry visibility instead of a hardcoded “current” badge forever. CTA opens official terms, not a simulated purchase flow implying this demo qualifies as the participating sales channel. Unverified/expired entries are excluded from active promotions, not silently relabeled as discounts. This research did not submit applications, redeem rewards, or use customer data.
